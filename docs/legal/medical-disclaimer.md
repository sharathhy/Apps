# Medical Disclaimer (DRAFT — for legal review)

This app does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Consult a qualified healthcare professional.

- Information in the app is general and for wellness purposes only. Where health content is shown, its source (such as WHO, ACOG or NHS) is cited.
- Predictions such as your next period, fertile window, ovulation day and due date are **estimates** based on averages and the data you enter. They are not a method of contraception and are not a diagnosis.
- The app is not a medical device and has not been evaluated or cleared by any regulator (including CDSCO or the FDA).
- If you think you may have a medical emergency, contact your doctor or local emergency services immediately. In India call 112; in the USA call 911.
- If you are in distress or thinking about self-harm: India — Tele-MANAS 14416; USA — call or text 988.
